# The Sandbox is retired — 2026-08-15

This exists so nobody rebuilds it, and so nobody has to reconstruct why from the git history.

## The decision

The Sandbox verification factory — the divergence corpus, the four-part gate, the per-row anchor, check
spec, measurement and independent review — is **retired**. Compositions are now built from verified
primitives, looked at by a human in light and dark, and shipped.

## What it cost, measured

| | |
|---|---|
| Components already ported through the batch method | **60**, in batches of 10–24 per commit |
| Compositions shipped through the Sandbox | **0** |
| `rail-sidebar` elapsed | ~4 days |
| `rail-sidebar` divergence rows | 169 |
| Rows resolved at retirement | 18, of which **12 were overstated** (no anchor, unreachable by `--stale`) |
| Artifacts the gate demanded for one component | ~676 (169 × anchor + spec + evidence + review) |

The target is ~200 components in three weeks — about 36 minutes each. The Sandbox priced one at four
days. That is the whole argument; nothing else about it needed to be wrong.

## Why it failed, precisely

**It was aimed at the wrong unit of work.** The unit became the divergence row, not the component. Every
cross-cutting change then became a broadcast invalidation: landing one system change staled 23 evidence
rows and cost 12 review-ready rows in a single command.

**The corpus was authored before the machinery that had to satisfy it.** 169 rows were imported, then the
gate was designed around them, then anchors, then specs, then `decision.present`, then `token.authored`.
Each new requirement retroactively invalidated finished work.

**The rows were never homogeneous and the gate never learned that.** 62 were the build's own bug log —
already found, already fixed. ~90 were "we match origin here." ~12 were real open decisions. Only the
last group needed ceremony; all three got it.

**Self-verification failed repeatedly.** Four separate checks were found passing while the thing they
covered was broken. Every real defect in this project was caught by a human looking at a render.

**The real cost driver was the contract, not the component.** 169 rows existed because the rail was
matched against a foreign design difference-by-difference, each deviation needing justification, evidence
and review. The 60 primitives cost almost nothing because the rule there was "pull them in unchanged".
A composition built from verified primitives has nothing to reconcile against.

## What was kept, because it is cheap and earns its place

- **`tokens/` + the light/dark parity gate.** Authoring is centralised and the emitter refuses a token
  defined in one mode only.
- **`scripts/check-rules.mjs`** — machine-checked source rules, blocking in CI, zero per-component cost.
- **`scripts/check-quarantine.mjs`** and the `origin/` boundary.
- **The 60 verified primitives.** This is the asset. It is what makes a composition cheap.
- **The engineering lessons** — scroll-region pattern, icon rules, the short build checklist — now in
  `CLAUDE.md` at a length someone will actually read.

## What was retired

- The divergence corpus as a gate (it survives as a read-only record of the rail port).
- Per-row anchor / check spec / measurement / independent review.
- The staleness sweep as a blocker.
- Multi-machine ownership ceremony (`HANDOFF.md` machine sections, `usp_transfer_component`) — real
  engineering, disproportionate to a one-person project.
- The review card and queue as a workflow driver.

The database and `sandbox/` are left in place, read-only. Nothing new should be written to either.

## Standing instruction

**Do not rebuild this.** If a future session finds itself proposing per-claim evidence, a divergence row,
an anchor, or an independent-review step for a composition, that is this system reappearing under another
name. The check is one question: *does this cost more per component than looking at the render?* If yes,
it is the same mistake.

## Known-inaccurate records left behind

12 rows in the corpus are marked `resolved` but have no anchor or declaration: `C-6 C-7 C-8 C-9 D-1 D-3
D-4 D-5 D-7 E-7 G-1 G-3`. Their evidence and reviews are genuine; the gate never required a declaration,
so it approved them and `--stale` can never reach them again. They are recorded here rather than repaired,
because repairing them fixes a display inside a retired system.
